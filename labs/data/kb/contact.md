# Contact us

## Support channels and hours
- **Live chat** in the app and website: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A virtual assistant answers first and can transfer you to an agent.
- **Phone**: 8:00 to 22:00, Saturday to Thursday.
- **Email**: replies within 24 hours.

Support is available in Arabic and English.

## Talking to a human agent
In live chat, type "agent" or choose **Talk to an agent** at any time. Average waiting time is under 5 minutes. For complaints about a previous conversation, ask the agent for a case number so the conversation can be reviewed.
