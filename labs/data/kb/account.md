# Your account

## Creating an account
Sign up with your mobile number or email address. You will receive a one-time password (OTP) to verify it. You can also sign up with Apple or Google. One mobile number can be linked to only one account.

## Registration problems
If you do not receive the OTP, wait 60 seconds and request a new one (maximum 5 attempts per hour). Check that the number is entered without the leading zero after +966. If your number is already linked to another account, use **Forgot password** to recover that account instead of creating a new one.

## Editing your account
Change your name, email, mobile number, addresses and communication preferences under **Account → Profile**. Changing the mobile number or email requires OTP verification on the new contact.

## Switching account type
You can switch between a personal and a business account under **Account → Account type**. Switching to business requires a commercial registration (CR) number and, optionally, a VAT number. Order history is kept when switching.

## Recovering your password
Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter your mobile number or email. The reset link or OTP is valid for 30 minutes. After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes for security.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under **Account → Privacy → Delete account**. Deletion is completed after a 30-day grace period, during which you can cancel the request by signing in. Open orders, returns and wallet balances must be settled first. Some records, such as tax invoices, are retained as required by law. You can also request a copy of your personal data from the same page, in line with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
