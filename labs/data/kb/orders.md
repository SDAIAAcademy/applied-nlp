# Orders

## Placing an order
Add items to your cart, choose a delivery address and delivery option, select a payment method and confirm. You will receive an order confirmation email and SMS with an order number in the format A-12345. Guests can order without an account, but must use the link in the confirmation email to manage the order.

## Changing an order
You can change the items, quantities or delivery option of an order within 1 hour of placing it, as long as it is not yet marked "Processing". After that, cancel the order (if not shipped) and place a new one. The payment method cannot be changed after the order is placed, except for cash-on-delivery orders, which can be switched to card payment at the door.

## Tracking an order
Open **My Orders** and select the order to see its status and the courier tracking link. Statuses are: Placed, Processing, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after an order is marked "Shipped". If an order is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact us and we will open an investigation with the courier.
