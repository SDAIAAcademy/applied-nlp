# Feedback and complaints

## How complaints are handled
Every complaint receives a case number. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 5 business days. If you are not satisfied with the resolution, ask for escalation to a supervisor; escalated cases are reviewed within 2 business days. Urgent safety issues, such as a damaged electrical product, are prioritised and handled the same day.

## Product reviews
You can review any product you bought from **My Orders → Write a review**, starting 3 days after delivery. Reviews are published within 48 hours if they follow our review guidelines: no personal data, no offensive language, and relevant to the product.
