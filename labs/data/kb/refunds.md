# Refunds

DemoMart is a fictional online store used in this course. All policies below are invented for training purposes.

## Refund eligibility
Most items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Perishable goods, gift cards, downloadable software and personalised items are not eligible for a refund. Items marked "final sale" can be exchanged but not refunded.

## How to request a refund
Open **My Orders**, select the order, choose **Return or refund**, pick the items and the reason, and submit. You will receive a return label by email within 2 hours. Hand the package to any courier drop-off point within 7 days of receiving the label. Refunds for orders paid with cash on delivery are sent to your DemoMart Wallet or to a bank account (IBAN) you provide.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are issued after the returned item passes inspection, usually within 2 business days of arriving at our warehouse. After that:
- DemoMart Wallet: within 24 hours.
- mada, Visa and Mastercard cards: 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.
- Apple Pay and STC Pay: 3 to 5 business days.
- Bank transfer (IBAN): up to 10 business days.

## Tracking a refund
The status of a refund is shown under **My Orders → Returns**. Statuses are: Requested, In transit, Received, Approved, Refunded. If a refund shows "Refunded" but has not appeared in your account after the timeline above, contact your bank with the refund reference number shown on the order page, or contact us and we will send a bank confirmation letter.

## Partial refunds
If only some items in an order are returned, only those items are refunded. Original delivery fees are refunded only when the whole order is returned because of a fault, a wrong item, or damage in transit.
