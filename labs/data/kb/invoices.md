# Invoices

## Getting an invoice
A VAT-compliant tax invoice is issued for every order when it ships. Download it as a PDF from **My Orders → Invoice**. Business customers with a VAT registration number can add it under **Account → Business details** to receive tax invoices with their company name.

## Checking or correcting an invoice
Invoices show the invoice number (format INV-2024-00001), item prices including 15% VAT, delivery fees and discounts. If the company name, VAT number or address on an invoice is wrong, request a corrected invoice within 30 days of the order date by contacting us. Amounts cannot be changed on an issued invoice; price disputes are handled as refunds.
