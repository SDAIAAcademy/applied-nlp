# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery options
- **Standard delivery**: 3 to 5 business days to all cities in Saudi Arabia. Free for orders above 200 SAR, otherwise 19 SAR.
- **Express delivery**: 1 to 2 business days in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Makkah and Madinah. 35 SAR.
- **Same-day delivery**: in Riyadh and Jeddah for orders placed before 12:00 noon. 49 SAR.
- **Pickup point**: collect from a partner locker within 7 days. Free.

## Delivery periods
Delivery estimates are shown at checkout and in the confirmation email. Business days are Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays. During major sale events and during Ramadan, standard delivery may take up to 2 extra business days.

## Setting up a shipping address
Add addresses under **Account → Addresses**. A valid address needs a building number, street, district, city and postal code, or a national short address (for example RRRD2929). You can save up to 10 addresses and choose a default one.

## Changing the shipping address
The delivery address can be changed from **My Orders** until the order is marked "Shipped". After shipping, you can only redirect the parcel to a pickup point in the same city, using the courier link in the delivery SMS.

## International shipping
DemoMart currently ships to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman and Qatar. Customs duties for GCC destinations are included in the price shown at checkout.
