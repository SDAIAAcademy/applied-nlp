# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, STC Pay, DemoMart Wallet balance, and cash on delivery (for orders up to 1,000 SAR, with a 10 SAR fee). Instalment plans are available through approved partners for orders above 300 SAR.

## Payment failed
A payment may fail because of insufficient balance, an expired card, a 3-D Secure verification timeout, or a bank limit on online purchases. Your order is kept for 30 minutes so you can retry with the same or another method. If the payment failed but money was deducted, it is an authorisation hold that your bank will release automatically within 3 to 5 business days.

## Charged twice
If you see two charges for one order, one of them is usually a pending authorisation that will disappear within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges are still shown as completed after 5 business days, contact us with the order number and a bank statement screenshot; we will refund the duplicate charge within 2 business days of verification.

## Payment security
DemoMart never asks for your full card number, CVV or one-time password (OTP) by phone, email or chat. Report any such request to our support team immediately.
