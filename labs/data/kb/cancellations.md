# Cancellations

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order free of charge from **My Orders** until it is marked "Shipped". Cancellation is immediate and the payment authorisation is released by your bank, usually within 3 to 5 business days. Orders that are already shipped cannot be cancelled; instead, refuse the delivery or request a return after delivery.

## Cancellation fees
There is no fee to cancel an order before it ships. If you refuse a delivery of a shipped order without a valid reason (wrong, damaged or late item), a return-shipping fee of 15 SAR is deducted from the refund. Large items (furniture and large appliances) have a cancellation fee of 50 SAR once they are scheduled for delivery. Pre-orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time before release.

## Rescheduling a delivery
To reschedule instead of cancelling, use the link in the delivery SMS. Deliveries can be rescheduled up to two times.
