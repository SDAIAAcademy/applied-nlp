AI Engineering Track · Bootcamp

Applied NLP Engineering

From tokens to fine-tuned language models: build, evaluate and adapt practical NLP systems for English and Arabic text.

AI Engineering Track 3 teaching days × 4h + capstone day 12 training hours ~25% concepts · ~75% hands-on Python

One case study, all week

Intelligent Support Ticket Processing. Given an English or Arabic customer message, identify its intent, find similar historical cases, assign a category and priority, and draft a grounded recommended reply. Every lab adds a component to this system.

Represent

Tokenization, embeddings, transformers, decision models

Generate

LLMs, decoding, structured outputs, RAG, model selection

Adapt

Encoder fine-tuning, LoRA for LLMs, evaluation, serving

Demonstrate

Team capstones: live demo, results, technical Q&A

Capstone

Teams of 2–3 build an applied NLP solution, choosing one of:

  • Intelligent Support Ticket Assistant: classify, retrieve similar cases, draft grounded replies
  • Arabic Document Intelligence: extract and categorize; answer questions with evidence
  • Voice of Customer Analyzer: classify feedback, cluster issues, summarize insights

Submit a repository with working code, README, evaluation results and sample inputs/outputs. Template: labs/capstone/.

Rubric

Functional end-to-end solution30%
Model selection & implementation25%
Evaluation & error analysis20%
Fine-tuning or justified adaptation15%
Presentation & reproducibility10%

Participants will be able to

  • Explain and implement tokenization, embeddings and transformer inference
  • Build semantic search and text classification systems
  • Develop structured-output and retrieval-grounded LLM applications
  • Choose between discriminative decision models and generative LLMs
  • Fine-tune transformers with supervised and parameter-efficient methods
  • Evaluate, compare and demonstrate practical NLP solutions

Setup

Audience: Python-proficient developers and data scientists with basic ML. No prior transformer experience needed.

Environment: Google Colab (GPU) or local with uv. Run 00_setup_check before Day 1; see labs/README.md.

Stack: Hugging Face Transformers, Tokenizers, Datasets, PEFT · Sentence Transformers · FAISS · scikit-learn · PyTorch · Pydantic · Gradio · an OpenAI-compatible LLM endpoint (optional).
